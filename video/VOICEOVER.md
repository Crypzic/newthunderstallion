# Thunder Stallion explainer — voiceover script

Length: 60 seconds · about 140 words · calm, confident, slightly energetic delivery.
Each line is timed to what is on screen. Pause briefly at each `/`.

| Time | On screen | Say |
|---|---|---|
| 0:00 – 0:04 | Lightning strikes, the logo slams in | Thousands of tokens launch every day. / Most vanish unseen. |
| 0:04 – 0:10 | The live ThunderTrend board tilts in and scrolls | Thunder Stallion is where traders find what's moving — / live, across Solana, BSC, Ethereum and Robinhood. |
| 0:10 – 0:13 | Trenches | Catch fresh launches in the Trenches, |
| 0:13 – 0:15.5 | Hottest | see where the big money flows, |
| 0:15.5 – 0:18 | Search finds a token | and find any token in seconds. |
| 0:18 – 0:24 | A vote is cast; the Top 10 Hot Spot glows | The community votes — / and the strongest projects rise to the Hot Spot. |
| 0:24 – 0:30 | The submit form fills itself in | Got a project? List it free. / Paste your contract, and the details fill themselves in. |
| 0:30 – 0:36.5 | The five Thunder Trend tiers deal in | Want more eyes on it? Boost with Thunder Points — / five tiers, from a quick push to the full takeover. |
| 0:36.5 – 0:46 | Gold Thunder Ticker rows, the Elite spotlight, then the Elite Profile | Climb the board, glow gold with the Thunder Ticker, take the front-page spotlight — / or upgrade to an Elite Profile with your own banner. |
| 0:46 – 0:50.5 | Checkout, then "Order received" | Pick a package, pay in the crypto you hold, / and place your order. |
| 0:50.5 – 0:54 | Admin activates; the token flies to #1 | The moment payment is confirmed — / your token goes live. |
| 0:54 – 1:00 | Lightning, logo | Thunder Stallion. / Discover. Track. Ride. |

## Recording tips
- Record in a quiet room, phone or mic about a hand's width from your mouth.
- Read each line once on its own, leave a second of silence between lines; it is easier to place them in the edit.
- In CapCut (or any editor): drop the video in, add the voiceover underneath, slide each line to its start time above.
- Add a dark electronic track under it at low volume, with a hit on the lightning at 0:00, 0:54 and 0:53 (go live).
